How it works

You book in minutes. We take the evening.

You choose a chef, the dishes, the night and your guests, usually in under five minutes. Then it’s our turn.

  1. 3:30 pm
    Shops

    Groceries bought that day.

  2. 5:30 pm
    Arrives

    Two hours before service. Sets up in your kitchen.

  3. 7:30 pm
    Cooks & serves

    Each dish plated and brought to the table.

  4. 10:00 pm
    Clears away

    Your kitchen is left clean.

Pricing is each dish’s price per guest, plus a 12% service and groceries fee. Nothing is charged until your chef accepts.

About

Clubvivre brings restaurant-level cooking to your own table. A small roster of resident chefs, a short menu each, and everything handled: the groceries, the cooking, the plating and the clearing away.
