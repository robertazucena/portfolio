    Questions we hear first.

    Still unsure? Ask us on the contact page and a specialist will answer in plain words.

    Will solar work during the rainy season?

    Yes. Panels produce less under heavy cloud, but they still generate in daylight. We size systems on a full year of sunshine, so lower months are already counted in your estimate.

    Can I sell extra power back to the grid?

    Under the Philippine net-metering programme, systems up to 100 kW can export surplus power to the distribution utility for a bill credit. We prepare the application documents with you.

    Do I need a battery?

    Not always. If you are home in the evening or face frequent outages, a battery makes sense. If most of your use is during the day, panels alone usually give the faster payback.

    Is my roof strong enough?

    We check the structure, the age of the roofing and the mounting points during the site survey. If anything needs fixing first, we tell you before any quote.

    What happens during a typhoon?

    Mounts and fixings are chosen for local wind loads, and the system can be shut down safely. After a storm we can inspect the array for you.

    How long does installation take?

    Most homes are installed in one to three days after permits. Commercial and industrial projects are scheduled around your operations, often without a full shutdown.
