  1. 1932

    The American Association of Malaya first suggests a club. Only 56 Americans live in Singapore, too few to support one, so the plan waits.

  2. 1939

    A bid for the German Club building nearly gives the Club its first home. War breaks out, the property is confiscated and later destroyed.

  3. 1948

    On 14 September the Club opens with 146 Members on a rented fifth-floor balcony of the Cathay Building. There are two slot machines and five staff.

  5. 1955

    A California-style clubhouse opens at 21 Scott Road, with Singapore’s first bowling alley. With no air-conditioning, pin-boys reset every frame by hand.

  6. 1958

    The Manhattan Room opens, named for the mural of the New York skyline that covers one whole wall.

  8. 1963

    On 30 November the kidney-shaped pool opens, and the Club becomes a place for the whole family.

  9. 1978

    One acre of Claymore Hill is bought for $2.29 million for squash, racquetball, four tennis courts and 88 parking spaces.

  11. 1989

    The Claymore Hill Complex opens in May, home to the Liberty Lounge and a new sports bar called Union Bar.

  13. 2015

    A $65 million redevelopment replaces the Scotts Road building and pool, and renews Claymore Hill and the Sports Complex.

  14. Today

    More than 11,000 Members of 90+ nationalities, a member-owned club run with 25 volunteer committees. Still your home away from home.
