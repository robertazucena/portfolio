Easier than you’d think

Start with a visit

  1. Visit

    Take a tour, meet the team and ask anything you like over lunch.

  2. Apply

    Send your form and proof of citizenship or residency. Don’t know a Member to propose you? We’ll introduce you.

  3. Hear back

    The Membership Committee meets monthly. Most people have an answer within six weeks.

  4. Move in

    Collect your cards and join a new Members’ evening. Everyone there was new once.

Bring your friends along

Most new Members first came as someone’s guest. Introduce a friend who joins and you’re both rewarded with dining credit.

Good questions to ask us

Who can join?

Anyone aged 21 and above. Your citizenship and whether you are joining as a person or a company decide which categories are open to you. The finder on this page shows them.

Who is covered by my membership?

Your spouse and children under 21 use the Club on your membership, each with their own card. Older children can ask about staying on under the family account.

What is the difference between Ordinary and Transferable?

Ordinary is bought from the Club and stays with you for life. Transferable is bought from a current Member on the secondary market, and the Club charges a transfer fee.

Are there costs after joining?

Yes. All Members pay monthly dues, and what you spend at the Club goes on your Member account. We will walk you through the current dues on your tour.

Can I bring guests?

Yes. Members can sign in guests for meals and most facilities. Some areas have a small guest fee, and a guest must be with you while at the Club.

How long does it take?

Most applications are decided within six weeks of a complete submission. Term Membership currently has a waitlist, and Associate Membership is closed.
