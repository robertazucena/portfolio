What we keep

Your places, income, CPF and cash figures, shortlist, viewing requests, messages and offers. They're saved in your browser's local storage on this device only, and never sent anywhere.

What agents and owners see

When you book a viewing you choose whether to share your fit summary: your places, travel times and fit score. They see your name, your message and any offer you send.

They never see your income, CPF or savings.

Services we use

Map tiles and address search come from OneMap by the Singapore Land Authority, which receives the map area you view and any address you search. Fonts load from Google Fonts and the map library from cdnjs. None of them receive what you type into Nowhere.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising cookies, no tracking pixels.

Nowhere is a design concept. Everything here shows how a live service would work.
