How the ranking works

How is the fit score worked out?

It weighs the time you'd spend travelling to your places each week, door to door by MRT and on foot, against the cash you'd pay each month after CPF covers its share of the loan. Homes within 4 km of your parents get a lift for the Proximity Housing Grant, and homes within 1 km of your child's school get one for priority admission.

How do you know how much I can borrow?

We test the loan at 4% interest over 25 years against the 30% Mortgage Servicing Ratio for HDB and EC flats and the 55% Total Debt Servicing Ratio, capped at 75% of the price. It's an estimate, not financial advice.

Are the floor plan and walkthrough accurate?

They're a guide. The plan is drawn from the listing's size and room count, and the walkthrough is built from its photos. Rooms marked “similar” use a sample photo for scale. Ask the agent for the official plan before you renovate.

Agents and your data

Can agents see my income?

Never. When you book a viewing you choose whether to share your fit summary: your places, travel times and fit score. Your income, CPF and savings stay on your device.

How do I delete my data?

Everything lives in your browser on this device. The Privacy page shows what's stored right now and clears it in one tap.

Are the listings real?

No. Nowhere is a design concept, so every listing, agent, owner and price is a sample. The towns, MRT stations and the map are real.

Buying a home

How accurate are the grants and stamp duty?

They follow the 2026 HDB and IRAS rules in simplified form: the CPF Housing Grant, the Enhanced Housing Grant, the Proximity Housing Grant, and Buyer's and Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty. Use them to compare homes; HDB and your bank give the real figures.

Is an offer binding?

No. An offer sent on Nowhere isn't binding on you or the seller. Nothing is final until the seller grants you the Option to Purchase and you pay the option fee.

How do I list my home?

Choose “For agents & owners”, then “List a home”. Agents verify with their CEA registration and owners with Singpass. It takes about three minutes.
