What Nowhere is

Nowhere helps you compare homes for sale in Singapore against your own week and budget, book viewings, and make offers. It doesn't buy or sell property, and it isn't an estate agency.

Estimates, not advice

Fit scores, travel times, loan limits, grants, stamp duty and timelines are estimates. They're worked out from what you enter and from simplified public rules.

They aren't financial, legal or tax advice. Check with HDB, IRAS, your bank and a conveyancing lawyer before you commit.

Listings

Agents and owners are responsible for what they list. Agents must hold a valid CEA registration, and owners verify with Singpass.

Floor plans and walkthroughs are guides drawn from the listing. Ask for the official plan.

Viewings and offers

A viewing request is a request until the agent or owner confirms it. An offer sent through Nowhere isn't binding on you or the seller. A sale starts only when the seller grants an Option to Purchase and you pay the option fee.

Using Nowhere fairly

Don't post false or duplicate listings, impersonate an agent or owner, or contact a buyer about anything other than the home they asked about.

Changes

We may update these terms. The date at the top shows when they last changed.
