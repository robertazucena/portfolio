# Credits

The Greed concept car is based on "Fictional supercar - V12 Goblin"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fictional-supercar-v12-goblin-0a20e49ad5774d778567cb5c3f345786)
by ollitei (https://sketchfab.com/ollitei), licensed under CC-BY-4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: repainted in metallic black with red trim, textures desaturated, the
original badges removed, a GREED wordmark, G hub caps and a GRD 810 plate added,
and the car relit and rendered in Blender for Greed.
