04Questions

Asked often,
answered plainly.

01What logistics services does Howely offer?

Transportation, warehousing, inventory management, order fulfilment and customs clearance, as one connected service or separately.

02Can the services be customised?

Yes. Every setup is shaped to fit into the way your business already runs.

03How do you make a supply chain more efficient?

By streamlining the process end to end: shorter transit times, lower costs and fewer handoffs between suppliers.

04Do you handle international shipping?

Yes. We work with a network of partners to move shipments in and out of Singapore promptly and safely.

05Which industries do you serve?

E-commerce, retail, manufacturing and healthcare among others, including beauty, fashion, perishables, appliances and electronics brands.

06Can you handle large-scale operations?

Yes. We have the experience, resources and infrastructure for complex supply chains, from mid-range to high-level contract logistics.

07How do you keep goods secure in transit?

GPS tracking, secure warehousing and trained people handling your stock at every stage.
