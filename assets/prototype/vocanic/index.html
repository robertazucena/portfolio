One agency. Five cities. Many voices.

Asia’s largest full-service social media agency. Singapore-based, part of GroupM.

Founded in 2005.
Twitter came a year later.

  1. 2005

    Ian McKee starts a company.

    He comes from tech start-ups, business analytics and big data consulting, and he sets up in Singapore. Twitter does not exist yet. Facebook is still for students. Brands talking with people in public has no obvious home yet.

  2. 2010

    Word of mouth travels.

    The trade press now calls Vocanic a word of mouth and social marketing agency. New offices open in Hong Kong and Malaysia.

  3. 2013

    GroupM buys in. The name stays.

    Word of a deal goes round from October, when Marketing magazine first reports it. In December, WPP confirms it: Vocanic will sit under GroupM, its media investment arm, with a majority stake sold. The reports name no price. Vocanic has 70 people, offices in Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia and Thailand, and clients including StarHub, Unilever, Dell and Standard Chartered.

“This investment significantly accelerates the capabilities of our agencies to meet and anticipate client needs.”

Mark Patterson, CEO, GroupM Asia Pacific, December 2013

“…deliver large scale social media programs that integrate with a brand’s overall strategy and deliver significant results.”

Ian McKee, CEO, Vocanic, December 2013
What changed
  • WPP becomes majority owner
  • The team is due to move into GroupM’s offices
  • Social work for clients of GroupM’s four agencies
What stayed
  • The Vocanic name
  • Ian McKee and his management team
  • Its own clients, and its own pitches

The agency he started is the one on this page: 120+ people, five offices, still answering.
