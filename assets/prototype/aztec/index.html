01About Aztec

Making digital finance accessible to everyone.

Aztec is a new digital finance company. We're building one simple place to manage, access and understand your money, from everyday payments to cryptocurrency.

The problem

Money moved online. The tools didn't get easier.

Banking apps are crowded, crypto apps speak in jargon, and fees, networks and risks are rarely explained in plain words.

What it costs people

Some stay away. Others jump in blind.

Without clear information, people either miss out on useful tools or sign up for things they don't fully understand.

What Aztec does

Products that explain themselves.

Clear words, honest numbers, and every cost and risk shown before we ask you to do anything.

What guides us

Five values we check every product decision against. Pick one to see what it means for you, or watch the dial turn through them.
