about · since 1976

The merger behind ON Engineers

ON Engineers is the merger of two electrical consultancy companies, J. M. Pang & Seah Pte Ltd (JMPS) and Quality Power Management Pte Ltd (QPM) — now part of the SWTS Asia group.

our history

Two consultancies, one merged practice

1976

JMPS was established

J. M. Pang & Seah Pte Ltd begins electrical consultancy work in Singapore.

2000

QPM was established

Quality Power Management Pte Ltd founded, focused on switchgear and power system maintenance.

2012

JMPS acquired by PON

J. M. Pang & Seah Pte Ltd is acquired by PON from Holland.

2017

QPM acquired by PON

Quality Power Management Pte Ltd is acquired by PON from Holland.

2019

JMPS & QPM merge

The two consultancies merge to become ON Engineers.

2022

Joins SWTS Asia

ON Engineers becomes part of the SWTS Asia group.
