03Working method

Four moves. No black box.

We make progress visible, invite the right friction early, and leave teams with a system they can own.

  1. 01 1–2 weeks

    Frame

    Define the consequential problem, users, evidence and a measurable definition of useful.

  2. 02 2–3 weeks

    Find

    Research behaviour and systems; model the opportunity before polishing the answer.

  3. 03 3–6 weeks

    Form

    Prototype content, interactions and technology together until the whole system clicks.

  4. 04 4–12 weeks

    Field

    Ship in slices, instrument outcomes and improve with the people who use it.
