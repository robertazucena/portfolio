How After Works

Read. Feel. Own.

01

Read the chapter

Every release opens as a story first. Scroll through it for free — no account, no cart, nothing to buy. Some visitors never go further, and that's the point.

02

Feel the mark

Each chapter leaves a single visual mark — a ripple, a trace, a residue. That mark becomes the print. Nothing on the shirt is decoration; it's a fragment of the story.

03

Own the artifact

Fifty pieces per size, printed once, never restocked. When a chapter sells out, it closes for good — the same way a chapter of a book can't be rewritten.

What to Know First

Exactly fifty pieces per size, per chapter. When a size sells out, it's marked as such on this page and never restocked — the inventory counter above is live, not decorative.

S through XXL, cut as a relaxed unisex fit. Use the Size Guide link next to the size selector for a full chest and length breakdown before you buy.

Orders ship within 2 business days and arrive in 3–7 days depending on destination. Every artifact travels in packaging designed as part of the chapter, not an afterthought.

Cold wash, inside out, hang dry. The garment is stone-washed before printing, so it's meant to soften with time rather than be babied.
