About

After is not a clothing brand. It's a collection of stories you can wear.

Read it.Wear it.Keep it.

After is built one chapter at a time, by people who'd rather tell you one true thing slowly than a hundred things fast. There is no season calendar and no drop schedule — a chapter is released when it's finished, not before.

How a chapter becomes a shirt
01

Write

A short chapter is written first — the story always comes before the shirt.

02

Distill

One image from the story is chosen — a single mark that carries the whole chapter.

03

Print

The mark is screen-printed once per garment — fifty shirts, per size, no more.

04

Release

Chapter and garment go live together. When it sells out, it closes for good.
